# Course report (LaTeX)

LaTeX version of the Notion page *Agent-Driven Design Space Exploration for HLS
Acceleration of SRCNN on the Kria KV260* (course: Hardware Accelerated
Computing), converted on 2026-10-26 from the page as last edited on 2026-10-25 19:15 UTC.

## Build

```bash
bash report/build.sh        # tectonic (XeTeX) if installed, otherwise latexmk -xelatex
```

The document also compiles with pdfLaTeX (`latexmk -pdf main.tex`), e.g. on
Overleaf; the preamble selects fonts by engine. Required packages are standard
TeX Live ones (fontspec or tgpagella, booktabs, tabularx, pdflscape, listings,
caption, hyperref, microtype).

## Layout

`main.tex` starts with a `\compacttrue` / `\compactfalse` switch. Compact (the
default) is 10 pt with 2 cm margins and reduced figure sizes, which puts the
body (Introduction to Conclusion) on 25 pages; the roomier setting is 11 pt
with 2.4 cm margins (about 38 body pages). The text is identical in both.


| Path | Content |
|---|---|
| `main.tex` | Preamble, title, table of contents, `\input` of the chapters and appendices |
| `sections/01-…07-*.tex`, `08-references.tex` | One file per chapter, in the order of the Notion page; the References list becomes a `thebibliography` and `[n]` citations become `\cite` |
| `sections/A-evolution.tex` | Appendix A: the complete 207-experiment graph (`figures/evolution/full.pdf`, recovered from git history, `docs/experiment-evolution/full.en.svg` at commit def95cc) and two of the seven per-phase graphs (`figures/evolution/*.pdf`, vector exports of `experiments/evolution/*.svg`); the other five are present but commented out |
| `skill/SKILL.md`, `sections/B-skill.tex` | Appendix B: the English `experiment-exploration` skill, copied from `~/myagent/skills/experiment-exploration-en/SKILL.md` and rendered with `tools/build_skill_appendix.py` (pandoc) |
| `code/C17_readable_srcnn.cpp` | Notion sub-page "Readable version code" (Appendix C). The "Original Code" sub-page is not reproduced (authors' decision); it is `designs/winograd_t4_windows32_v1/srcnn.cpp` in the repository |
| `figures/fig00-network.png` | Network figure supplied separately (not on the Notion page); inserted as Figure 1 of the Introduction |
| `figures/` | The 11 images of the page; `fig01` is the vector export of the identical `docs/figs/experiment-loop.svg`, `fig04` and `fig06` are vector exports of the identical SVGs in `docs/figures/C17/notitle/` |
| `tools/notion2tex.py`, `tools/notion-page.md` | The converter and the Notion export it was run on |

The `.tex` files in `sections/` are generated by the converter
(`python3 tools/notion2tex.py tools/notion-page.md .`), including the few edits
the Notion markup cannot express (sub-page links, one caption paragraph, the
wiki bookmark as a footnote, Listing 2; see `fixups()` in the script).
Re-running the converter overwrites them, so hand edits should go either into
the script or be made after deciding not to re-run it.

## Differences from the Notion page

- Figure, table, listing and reference numbers are assigned by LaTeX; all
  cross-references and citations in the text are `\ref`/`\cite`, so they follow
  automatically.
- The Introduction's "Figure 1 shows the network" has no figure on the page;
  `figures/fig00-network.png` was supplied separately and is inserted there.
- References 4 (FSRCNN, with its sentence in the Introduction), 9 (Amidi
  cheatsheet), 14 (Claude Code / Codex), 17 (Xilinx INT8 white paper) and 19 (Zynq UltraScale+ TRM) and their
  citations are dropped at the authors' request; the
  numbering is reassigned by LaTeX. The Notion page still carries them.
- The Discussion refers to "Listing 2", which does not exist on the page; the
  loader loop from the original source was inserted as Listing 2.
- Of the two code sub-pages only the readable version is reproduced (Appendix C).
  To shorten the PDF, comment out its `\lstinputlisting` line at the end of `main.tex`.
