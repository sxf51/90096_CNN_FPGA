## 6. Interpretation / 解读

C17 passes all 13 course-provided images bit-for-bit against E065's mixed Conv2-w8 reference. Mean deltaPSNR is -0.07589037644656216 dB, within the unchanged -0.1 dB limit; butterfly six-pixel border MSE is 1.405191888267716e-05. The runtime float parameter interface and quantization points are unchanged. This establishes numerical equivalence for the depth32 window FIFO, while C simulation's unbounded streams do not establish finite-FIFO behavior or absence of RTL deadlock.

The three MAC folds achieve the requested II=1. HLS reports 1032 DSP, 146 BRAM18K, 127985 LUT and 192308 FF, with a 263090-cycle lower bound identical to C13. Preserve the original HLS infeasible status: these LUT/FF counts exceed the original HLS gates. They satisfy the user-approved screen for independent routing, which previously admitted C13 and led to the route-feasible C18. No physical feasibility or 1.052360 ms frame claim follows from this HLS estimate.

The paper estimates 1032 DSP, 146 BRAM18K, 129066 LUT and 193108 FF are close to the HLS totals. However, the incremental FIFO storage estimate of 1100 LUT becomes only 19 additional HLS LUT relative to C13. The logical depth increase is 30 * 1728 = 51840 bits; HLS stream accounting is not a physical storage measurement. F51 already demonstrates that unchanged HLS FIFO area can accompany a measurable whole-design routed LUT increase. Require C22's physical hierarchy to resolve this discrepancy rather than claiming free buffering.

The paper 272000-cycle prediction targets actual RTL by recovering C14's roughly 29083 per-frame window-wait cycles. HLS cannot observe those finite-FIFO stalls, so its unchanged 263090 bound neither validates nor rejects that prediction. C20 will measure the two exact changed-parameter transactions and process/FIFO counters. C22 is separately planned at 5 ns using the C18-validated staged Vivado backend, but remains unlaunched until C20 passes. Compare only matching-parent accepted RTL cycles times independently closed routing with C18's 1.503160 ms. No board or DDR result is inferred.
