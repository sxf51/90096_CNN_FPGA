## 6. Interpretation / 解读

F28 passes every unchanged WS2 gate against F25. Explicitly disabling local-load loop flattening restores the global estimate to 2.377ns while keeping all three continuous folds at II1, zero weight-predecessor warnings, exact butterfly output and unchanged six-pixel border MSE. Total 1,046,029 cycles remains below 1,067,473.04; outside LUT stays 3,170 and top Multiplexer stays 184.

The isolated control change costs 316 cycles relative to F27's 1,045,713, while total LUT falls from 61,656 to 61,553 and FF increases by six. Thus the repeated load-loop drain/startup is small enough to preserve the throughput improvement. Actual LUT and cycles differ from the advance F28 estimates by less than one percent; no estimate deviation above twenty percent is hidden.

Compared with F25, the final total-LUT reduction is 8,423, not the whole 29,124 reduction of the outside attribution. Loaders and private-bank control moved inside DATAFLOW, whose reported LUT is now 58,383. Keep total-area and ownership measurements separate when budgeting T8. DSP502 and BRAM18K73 are unchanged.

The archived F94 validation has identical source SHA256, precision and non-testbench macros to F28 and passes all thirteen supplied image pairs with deltaPSNR -0.0695141811880653dB. Select F28 as the strict WS2 loader and T16 fallback. F31 proves the independent changed-parameter testbench in C; F93 must still prove both transactions in finite-FIFO RTL. Missing comic and board validation remain unresolved.

Continuous scheduling separates window generation and MAC folds, shifts channel registers and explicitly registers fabric reduction trees. This can remove pixel fill/drain but costs pipeline registers and wide window FIFOs. Online padding overlaps source reads with padded writes; old row serialization can create finite-FIFO stalls absent from maximum-component HLS estimates. RTL cycles and routed slack remain the final evidence.
