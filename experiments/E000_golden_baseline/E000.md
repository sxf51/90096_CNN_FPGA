## 6. Interpretation / 解读

The full feature maps are mapped to 11,064 BRAM18K (3,841.67% of the 288-block budget), so this implementation is infeasible regardless of its ten-DSP footprint. The synthesis estimate is 1,960,113,853 cycles or 19,601.13853 ms at the target 10 ns. Default automatic scheduling creates Conv1 MAC II 5, Conv2 pixel II 32 and Conv3 MAC II 4: float accumulation recurrence and array-port scheduling prevent one-cycle MAC throughput. Estimated clock 7.331 ns is slightly above the 7.30 ns clock after uncertainty, also reflected by -0.03 ns slack in the report. These are HLS estimates, not implementation or board measurements.
