# E031: dataflow_fixed_T32

## 1. Purpose and hypothesis / 目的与假设

Synthesize first streaming fixed architecture at T=32 after exact butterfly agreement with the actual-ap_fixed-boundary reference. Ideal multiplication budget 162+64+25=251 DSP, compute floor 2,080,800 frame cycles plus weight load, padding and pipeline overhead; these are predictions, not measured results.

Conv1 fold module latency is 52.0 cycles, with interval 33.0. Its containing spatial engine takes 3873991.0 cycles; Conv2 takes 2080820.0 cycles. Inner-loop II=1 alone does not establish T cycles per pixel: pipeline fill/drain and unpipelined spatial control add frame overhead.

The baseline report lists narrow `mul_10s_10s_20_1_1` fabric products at 62 LUT and zero DSP each, with a few fused DSP MACs. Complete register weight partitions also create large variable-index sparse muxes. Saturating every partial addition prevents associative reduction; Conv3's recurrence can satisfy II=1 by lengthening the combinational clock path. These effects explain the deviation from the one-DSP-per-MAC paper model.
