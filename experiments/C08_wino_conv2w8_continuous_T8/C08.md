E065 independently verifies Conv2-only signed8 weights/bias at the mixed precision point with13-image deltaPSNR=-0.07589037644656216dB. Pair those coefficients with shared ten-bit activations in one27x10 DSP product, with borrow correction but no coefficient residual restoration. Combine continuous integer Winograd Conv1 and direct Conv3; this avoids the failed uniformINT8 gate and full exact10 packing's large residual fabric cost.
