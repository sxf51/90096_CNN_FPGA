## 6. Interpretation / 解读

Conv3 ten-bit raw coefficients can fit signed8 while retaining fraction9; this is exact recoding, unlike the failed eight-bit fraction7 quantization. Conv2 accepts actual eight-bit weights (E065/E066), but its activations remain ten-bit with nonnegative nine-bit magnitudes. Such packing requires unpacking after at most two terms at gap18; the seven-term INT8 bound cannot be reused. G2 increases weight range without adding rounding; inverse transform must divide exactly by four. Paper budgets omit transforms, input loading, raster reorder, FIFO stalls and address/control overhead.

## 7. Conclusion and next step / 结论与下一步

Test DSP mapping, pre-adder use and exact unpacking in phase-two microbenchmarks before integrating. Raw weight residuals must be handled explicitly where needed. Full Set14 acceptance remains pending because comic is absent.
