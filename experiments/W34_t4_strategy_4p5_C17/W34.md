## 6. Interpretation / 解读

The final post-route physical optimization report fails setup: WNS=-0.324ns and TNS=-51.194ns over655 endpoints. Hold passes at+0.032ns and every net is routed. DSP1032 and BRAM18K109 match the C22 paper estimates exactly; LUT59893 versus59552 is+0.57%, and FF101443 versus100499 is+0.94%. No resource deviation exceeds20%, but4.5ns closure was not achieved;273023*4.5/1e6 remains a rejected hypothesis, not a measured frame time.

The five final setup paths in [critical_paths.csv](critical_paths.csv) all lie inside `continuous_wino_fold`, from result registers to `trunc_ln358` output/quantization registers. They have8-11 logic levels and4.792-4.822ns data-path delay. The worst path is1.337ns logic plus3.485ns routing (72.272% routing), so the measured bottleneck is Winograd arithmetic/output conversion with substantial routing, not solely parameter distribution. A balanced registered tree needs independent evidence that these paths improve.

[implementation/thread_observation.json](implementation/thread_observation.json) shows actual child `general.maxThreads=1`: applying the strategy after the thread hook cleared its step property, and reopening the staged project did not replay it. W34 remains the actual single-thread physical result. The repair in e684bbb applies only to fresh independent jobs; this archive must not be described as a four-thread strategy comparison. The complete course-provided Set14 set contains13 images, and no board measurement exists.
