# E046: dataflow_wrap_T32

## 1. Purpose and hypothesis / 目的与假设

Remove redundant saturation logic from exact fractional accumulators. The E010 analytical absolute-sum bounds fit integer bits 6/6/7 for the protected trained weights and every representable activation. Ideal budget 251 DSP, 2,080,800 compute cycles, unchanged buffers.
