## 6. Interpretation / 解读

Exact ten-bit coefficient residuals add fabric control/adders, and adjacent-pixel Conv3 needs a5x6 window plus odd-column disposal. Extra multiplier/pipeline depth is paid on every pixel in nested fold schedules. PE cycle counts cannot be interpreted as network-frame times.
