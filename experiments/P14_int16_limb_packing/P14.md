# P14: int16_limb_packing

## 1. Purpose and hypothesis / 目的与假设

Split every signed16 value as unsigned low8 plus256*signed high8. Four packed byte-product pairs reconstruct both complete32-bit products exactly. Each low byte product fits a signed18 field, including unsigned255*255=65025. Expect four DSP48E2 multipliers per pair: exact generic16-bit limb packing alone loses DSP area versus two direct products.

## 6. Interpretation / 解读

A complete signed16x16 product needs32 bits. Nonoverlapping two-product fields need at least gap32, making the coefficient port48 bits, beyond DSP48E2's27-bit input; splitting a coefficient alone leaves8x16 products needing24 bits and a32-bit packed port. P14 therefore splits both operands. P15 explicitly pays fabric recovery for overlapping fields. Count every DSP/fabric operation, compare P13/P16 controls, and retain failures or missed II without changing numerical gates.

## 7. Conclusion and next step / 结论与下一步

Compare complete32-bit product equality, DSP, LUT, FF, achieved II and clock. A one-DSP mixed implementation still spends LUT multipliers; integrate only after a demonstrated network budget.
