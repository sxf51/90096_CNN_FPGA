## 6. Interpretation / 解读

Compare requested932/1188/466 MAC DSP budgets with actual resources, especially residual fabric, transformed accumulation and raster buffering. More than20% deviation needs per-module evidence.

The combination retains Winograd Conv1 and uses packing only on other layers, avoiding incompatible transformed INT8 operands. Multiplier budgets omit residual/borrow correction, inverse arithmetic, runtime transformed weights and raster reorder. Dedicated layer resource counts must be summed once; nested child reports repeat their parents' resources.

Explicit explanation of deviations above20%:

Packing preserves ten-bit precision by restoring signed-low8 coefficient residuals and correcting negative borrowing. Its one-DSP pair therefore still needs wider fabric arithmetic, selection and pipeline stages. The nested fold loops restart at every pixel/tile, charging their full iteration latency in addition to T; pure-MAC budgets omitted that drain cost. The reported layer LUTs and loop depths below quantify the excess. Uniform INT8 cannot remove this logic without failing the unchanged precision gate.

The nested Conv1 tile producer reports 2370193.0 cycles. Its264x264 scan contains a conditional4*T fold, and the maximum bound charges that branch at every scanned coordinate; only128x128=16384 coordinates actually produce tiles. Consequently the reported worst-case bound is inflated, even with the requested fold II. W10-W13/C07 isolate this by replacing the nested branch with a fixed16384*(4*T) MAC loop. This is report-bound tightening until RTL cycles confirm an actual throughput change.

Observed fold depth/II: wino_fold depth=11, II=1.0; c2_pixels_c2_fold depth=10, II=1.0; c3_fold depth=6, II=1.0.
