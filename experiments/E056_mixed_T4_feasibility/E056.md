# E056: mixed_T4_feasibility

## 1. Purpose and hypothesis / 目的与假设

Measure T=4 feasibility with 648 Conv1 and 512 Conv2 DSP products (1160 total), and 648 Conv1 plus 200 Conv3 fabric products. Ideal frame compute floor 260,100 cycles. At E033's 62 LUT per fabric 10x10 multiply the 848 fabric products alone cost about 52,576 LUT before adders, weight muxes and AXI; the 81,984 total-LUT cap may therefore be exceeded.

## 2. Changes / 改动

Independent dataflow_dsp copy, T=4; first half of unrolled Conv1 output lanes bind to DSP, second half to fabric. Conv2 stays DSP and Conv3 uses fabric. Banked LUTRAM follows measured E039 storage evidence. All casts and no-overflow accumulators match E019/E017. Source commit: `fcd3ba401b3ba37f18e450be506eca80efa62521`.
