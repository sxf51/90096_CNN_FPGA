# F27: local_streamed_params_T16

## 1. Purpose and hypothesis / 目的与假设

WS2 ownership experiment: one parameter AXI-reading process inside DATAFLOW quantizes each of 8032 weights and97 biases once, then sends three distinct streams of5248/2080/801 values. Each engine loads its private cyclic simple-dual-port bank and biases before reading pixels. Hypothesize that shared caller/consumer bank-port multiplexers, duplicated converters and weight-related HLS200-1449 warnings disappear, reducing total LUT as well as outside-LUT attribution. Separate per-layer streams prevent interleaved parameter/pixel deadlock.

## 6. Interpretation / 解读

Ownership changes remove five caller/predecessor warnings and reduce top Multiplexer14134 to184 LUT. Outside32294 to3170 passes the12000 target, but DATAFLOW grows37682 to58486 because local storage/control moved into its engines. The meaningful total-area reduction is69976 to61656:8320LUT (11.89%), not the29124 outside difference.

Measured61656 is29.26% above the47700 paper estimate. Private banks retain local write decoding and load/compute selection: engine1 grows8753 to19304, conv2_stream3629 to8009 and engine3 12992 to14675, with read_params4057. Compute pipeline children remain near their old sizes at8726/3602/12970 LUT. These are hierarchical resources, so do not sum child pipelines with engine totals. The outside estimate3500 was within9.43%; retained inside bank/control overhead explains the failed total estimate.

All folds are II1;1045713cycles is1.06% below F25 and0.60% below paper. Butterfly is bit exact and border unchanged. Only the WS2 clock gate fails:2.419>2.377ns. HLS200-1016 locates engine3_Pipeline_local_load_channels_local_load_taps: tap load,0.789ns compare,0.414ns select,0.789ns increment and0.427ns store. read_params1.892ns, online padding2.377ns and Conv3 compute2.357ns show the flattened local-load counter is the new limit. F28 independently disables only that loader's loop flattening. F27 remains globally feasible/provisional but cannot be called WS2 accepted; C simulation does not prove finite-FIFO or changed-parameter RTL progress.

Continuous scheduling separates window generation and MAC folds, shifts channel registers and explicitly registers fabric reduction trees. This can remove pixel fill/drain but costs pipeline registers and wide window FIFOs. Online padding overlaps source reads with padded writes; old row serialization can create finite-FIFO stalls absent from maximum-component HLS estimates. RTL cycles and routed slack remain the final evidence.
