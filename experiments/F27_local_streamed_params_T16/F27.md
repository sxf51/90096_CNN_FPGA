# F27: local_streamed_params_T16

## 1. Purpose and hypothesis / 目的与假设

WS2 ownership experiment: one parameter AXI-reading process inside DATAFLOW quantizes each of 8032 weights and97 biases once, then sends three distinct streams of5248/2080/801 values. Each engine loads its private cyclic simple-dual-port bank and biases before reading pixels. Hypothesize that shared caller/consumer bank-port multiplexers, duplicated converters and weight-related HLS200-1449 warnings disappear, reducing total LUT as well as outside-LUT attribution. Separate per-layer streams prevent interleaved parameter/pixel deadlock.
