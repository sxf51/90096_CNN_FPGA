## 6. Interpretation / 解读

The `conv12_csynth.rpt` FIFO table confirms `features_U` width640 and depth16. Total644DSP/146BRAM18K/81086LUT/130057FF and525237HLS cycles match W21; all three compute folds achieveII1. Against the paper644DSP/146BRAM18K/81500LUT/130500FF allowance, resource differences remain below1%. The unchanged HLS cycle bound cannot resolve the finite-FIFO backpressure that added33521cycles in F97.

F44 independently tests two RTL transactions, including changed runtime weights/biases, and records channel/process stalls. WS-D's2% goal requires no more than535741 integer cycles; passing arithmetic/deadlock checks alone does not establish that goal. Compare it with depth32 F45 before selecting a FIFO size, and obtain routed storage/timing evidence before claiming physical feasibility.
