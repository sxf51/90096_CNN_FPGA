## 6. Interpretation / 解读

Full unrolling maps 60 of 64 constant multipliers to DSPs, with 424.0 LUT and zero FF. The Expression table contains 4 31-LUT subtractors; the source repeats -127 four times, consistent with shift/subtract realization of that coefficient. Constant folding therefore does not generally remove DSPs for these non-power-of-two coefficients. Worst-case latency is legitimately zero for this combinational top; interval is one. The parser now accepts zero-cycle reports after all resource and numerical checks.
