# W22: ws_clock_4p95_W21

## 1. Purpose and hypothesis / 目的与假设

Test one conservative physical-clock improvement on the exact W21 RTL that independently closed5ns in F98. F98's own first five paths identify three Conv2 parameter-FIFO-to-private-LUTRAM write-data paths and two Conv3 step-control-to-DSP-input paths, with routing-dominated delay and no DSP cascade. Tighten only the design clock constraint to4.95ns and measure timing/resources in a fresh workspace; preserve the5ns fallback. Also bound placement/routing to2 threads while keeping synthesis1 and the hardware lock serial. Runtime comparison is descriptive because clock and thread count both change; no isolated thread speedup claim is possible.
