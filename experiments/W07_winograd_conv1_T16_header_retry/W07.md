## 6. Interpretation / 解读

Measure transforms, wider arithmetic, runtime weight load, raster FIFO and pipeline fill/drain separately. Explain more than20% deviation from the multiplier-only budget.

Integer G2 preserves exact products and performs an exact divide-by-four before the original layer cast. Wider transforms and inverse additions, nine subkernels, runtime weight transforms and two raster row FIFOs are outside a multiplication-only budget. The128x128 tile grid computes65536 output positions before discarding the511 extra positions. PE/layer results do not establish full-network feasibility.

Explicit explanation of deviations above20%:

The nested Conv1 tile producer reports 5715601.0 cycles. Its264x264 scan contains a conditional4*T fold, and the maximum bound charges that branch at every scanned coordinate; only128x128=16384 coordinates actually produce tiles. Consequently the reported worst-case bound is inflated, even with the requested fold II. W10-W13/C07 isolate this by replacing the nested branch with a fixed16384*(4*T) MAC loop. This is report-bound tightening until RTL cycles confirm an actual throughput change.

Observed fold depth/II: wino_fold depth=11, II=1.0.
