# W18: narrow_raw_banks_T16

## 1. Purpose and hypothesis / 目的与假设

W09 and C12 measure transform_weights=40992 LUT/48601 FF at T8, with 48533 register FF and 19305 instance LUT in C12. W10 at T16 measures10459 LUT/495 FF. Original coefficients are read only during the sequential load/transform stage, so they need one output-channel bank rather than Conv1 MAC-lane banking. Remove only raw output-channel partitioning, retain all81 tap banks and every transformed compute bank. Test whether deeper64-entry LUTRAM avoids short-bank register/multiplexer growth; attribution remains a hypothesis until synthesis.

## 6. Interpretation / 解读

Compare transform_weights resources and top totals against W14/W15 and the measured W09/W10 controls. Distinguish raw-bank depth from transformed compute banks; check whether scheduling II or load cycles changed. A resource saving alone cannot establish frame feasibility or RTL throughput.

Integer G2 preserves exact products and performs an exact divide-by-four before the original layer cast. Wider transforms and inverse additions, nine subkernels, runtime weight transforms and two raster row FIFOs are outside a multiplication-only budget. The128x128 tile grid computes65536 output positions before discarding the511 extra positions. PE/layer results do not establish full-network feasibility.
