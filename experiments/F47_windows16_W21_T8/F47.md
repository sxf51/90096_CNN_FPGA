# F47: windows16_W21_T8

## 1. Purpose and hypothesis / 目的与假设

Change only the transformed-window FIFO in conv1_stream from2 to16 LUTRAM entries. F97's windows_U records45974 read-block cycles over two frames, while wino_engine has zero write stalls and its fold takes547283 rather than524288 nominal cycles. Hypothesis: a deeper window lookahead covers the no-tile scan row between paired tile rows, which a deeper raster feature FIFO may not address. Runtime weights, w10a10acc23, replicate padding and srcnn signature remain the frozen W21 contract. This is a separately measurable mainline control.

## 6. Interpretation / 解读

The `conv1_stream_csynth.rpt` FIFO table confirms `windows_U` depth16/width1728/27648bits versus W21 depth2/3456bits. HLS still assigns99FF/67LUT/0BRAM18K to this FIFO, exactly matching the shallower parent. Total644DSP/146BRAM18K/81086LUT/130057FF and all foldII1 are unchanged. Totals differ by-0.69% LUT/-0.42% FF from the paper81650LUT/130600FF allowance, within20%. The depth-insensitive FIFO resource estimate cannot establish the actual physical cost of adding24192bits; that remains a routed question.

F97's Winograd fold takes547283cycles versus524288 nominal and its transformed-window FIFO reports45974 read-block cycles over both transactions. The22995-fold excess per frame is close to45974/2=22987; these overlapping counters motivate a supply-gap hypothesis but do not prove causation. Sixteen windows cover512 compute cycles at32cycles/tile, compared with the roughly264-cycle scan-row gap. F48 tests whether deeper lookahead removes those read blocks and approaches the marginal535742-cycle2% target. Its changed-parameter RTL remains necessary even though all13 C outputs and borders are exact to E017.
