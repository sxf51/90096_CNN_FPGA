# W14: fixed_tiles_T8_allcontinuous_4ns_split4000

## 1. Purpose and hypothesis / 目的与假设

W11 C simulation passes exactly but synthesis rejects the8000-bit aggregated Conv3 stream with HLS214-256: maximum4096 bits. Preserve the continuous schedule and represent one800-element window as two25x16-channel4000-bit packets, each below the measured limit. Both streams produce/consume65025 packets per frame; no quantization or MAC change. W07 reports5715601 Conv1 cycles because the conditional nested264x264 scan takes its82-cycle tile branch at every position in the worst-case bound, even though only16384 tiles exist. Separate transformed-window production from a fixed16384*(4*T)II1 MAC loop; emit four-pixel blocks through a separate raster-row distributor. Use continuous direct Conv2/Conv3 and online padding as well, so their nested drains cannot dominate.

## 6. Interpretation / 解读

Compare the frozen estimates with parsed hardware reports. This is a representation fix for the compiler's4096-bit aggregate limit, not a precision relaxation. Check both stream counts and achievedII; final selected points still require full-supplied-image verification and finite-FIFO RTL cosimulation.

Integer G2 preserves exact products and performs an exact divide-by-four before the original layer cast. Wider transforms and inverse additions, nine subkernels, runtime weight transforms and two raster row FIFOs are outside a multiplication-only budget. The128x128 tile grid computes65536 output positions before discarding the511 extra positions. PE/layer results do not establish full-network feasibility.
