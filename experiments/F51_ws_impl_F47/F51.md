## 6. Interpretation / 解读

F51 independently passes all routed gates at 5 ns: WNS +0.055 ns, TNS 0, WHS +0.033 ns, no unrouted or partially routed nets, 644 DSP, 43617 LUT, 87203 FF, 109 BRAM18K and no URAM. F48 validated the identical F47 design in two changed-parameter RTL transactions at 535771 latency / 535800 interval cycles. The resulting IP frame time is 2.678855 ms. The cycle reduction versus F97 is 4.113943%; the time reduction versus W22's independently closed 4.95 ns point is 3.145400%. F48's original 2% diagnostic still misses by 30 cycles, while the user-authorized acceptance permits this improvement. C18 remains the fastest verified candidate; F51 at 5 ns is also slower than W30 at 4.5 ns.

The paper DSP estimate of 644 is exact. Routed/HLS LUT, FF and BRAM18K ratios are 0.537910, 0.670498 and 0.746575. These deviations exceed 20% because the estimates retain HLS scheduled operator/storage accounting, while Vivado optimizes the netlist and packs physical memory. The total physical LUT count is 1023 above F98's 42594 at the same 5 ns target, despite HLS reporting unchanged FIFO area. This resolves the earlier claim of apparently free FIFO depth at the whole-design level; it is not an isolated measurement of FIFO area, since implementation threading and physical mapping also differ. The copied synthesis/routed hierarchy gives the module breakdown without summing nested parent totals.

The first five setup paths in reports/impl/srcnn_setup5_routed.rpt all connect parameter FIFO registers to private coefficient LUTRAM: three in Conv3 and two in Conv2. They contain no DSP cascade and have zero intervening logic levels. The worst path is 4.791 ns, comprising 0.115 ns logic and 4.676 ns routing (97.600%), with data-net fanout 101. Deeper transformed-window buffering removes the measured window waits, but parameter distribution remains the physical clock bottleneck. A tighter clock requires a fresh implementation; the slack-derived 4.945 ns is not a validated target.

Implementation completed in 41.198177 tool minutes plus 0.653617 package minutes. The 1207 operation samples show 12.348110 GiB peak task-tree RSS, 2.045464 GiB minimum available memory and 15.968590% mean CPU busy across 12 logical CPUs, with no new OOM or protection termination. Legacy telemetry has no SwapTotal, so whole-run swap use remains unknown. The five paths were copied from the matching final generated report, with source/transfer hashes and stage recorded, without another Vivado run.
