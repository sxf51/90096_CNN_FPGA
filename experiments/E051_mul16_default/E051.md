## 6. Interpretation / 解读

The signed 16x16 multiply maps to 1 DSP in the Instance table. The 10 ns target achieves 2.38 ns estimated clock and samples II=1. The complete 64-sample kernel uses 62.0 LUT/16.0 FF, with 66.0 cycles and interval 64.0; these are PE-kernel measurements, not frame latency. C simulation verified 65536 deterministic random signed16 pairs.

## 7. Conclusion and next step / 结论与下一步

One DSP per scheduled signed16 multiply is supported at both tested clocks. Preserve this baseline for packing and Winograd PEs.
