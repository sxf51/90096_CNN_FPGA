## 6. Interpretation / 解读



Continuous scheduling separates window generation and MAC folds, shifts channel registers and explicitly registers fabric reduction trees. This can remove pixel fill/drain but costs pipeline registers and wide window FIFOs. Online padding overlaps source reads with padded writes; old row serialization can create finite-FIFO stalls absent from maximum-component HLS estimates. RTL cycles and routed slack remain the final evidence.

All 2 RTL transactions pass. Maximum latency is 1061153.0 cycles and interval is 1061182.0 cycles; latency differs from the frozen HLS bound by 1.1852510148056217%. Finite-FIFO/AXI handshake costs are included in RTL. This ideal-clock simulation does not establish routed timing or board latency.

The archived profiling/channels.csv and processes.csv locate FIFO backpressure and per-process stalls. Fast source/window producers are expected to block behind the T-fold MAC consumer; a large write-block fraction alone is not evidence that deeper FIFOs would improve frame throughput. Process counters span all recorded transactions and include startup/inactive cycles; overlapping stalls cannot be summed to explain the complete latency difference.
