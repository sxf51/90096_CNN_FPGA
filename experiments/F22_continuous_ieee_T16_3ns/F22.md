## 1. Purpose and hypothesis / 目的与假设

F20 exhausts all signed ten-bit output values across three orderings with exact binary32 equality and measures zero DSP, 242 LUT, 52 FF and 2.051ns. F13's output writer uses three DSP and the 3.239ns sitofp critical path. Replace only raw-to-float output encoding with the proven sign/exponent/significand construction, retain online replicate padding and Conv3 rotation, and test a faster target clock.
