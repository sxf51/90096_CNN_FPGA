## 6. Interpretation / 解读

All 13 course-provided images are bit exact against the independently verified w10a10 reference. Butterfly border MSE remains 1.661846957728205e-05; the mean PSNR change is -0.0695141811880653 dB, within the unchanged -0.1 dB gate. F35 separately proves all 12,161 generated coefficient/bias carriers equal the runtime-loaded banks.

The HLS estimate decreases from W21's 81,086 to 53,316 LUT and from 130,057 to 103,695 FF. This is consistent with removing the parameter converter, raw transform, parameter FIFOs and private write paths, but it does not establish the routed savings. The module report assigns 288 DSP to Conv1, 242 to Conv2 and 100 to Conv3: constant propagation removes 14 Conv2 products from the paper 644-DSP budget despite the retained DSP binding. The 630-DSP result differs by only 2.17%; BRAM18K 138 versus146, LUT 53,316 versus65,000 and FF 103,695 versus120,000 all remain within20% of the committed estimates.

All three continuous folds retain II=1. HLS latency falls only907 cycles, from525,237 to524,330; the HLS DATAFLOW model already overlaps parameter processes, so it cannot be used to confirm the paper prediction of about11k fewer RTL startup cycles. W25 measures that prediction independently with the same compile-time weights and a changed second input. The frame-time comparison remains unknown until both RTL and independent routing pass.
