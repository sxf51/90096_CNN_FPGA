# W19: streamed_winograd_T8

## 1. Purpose and hypothesis / 目的与假设

After strict WS2 loader selection, integrate its stream-owned parameter loading into independent W17 Winograd T8. Preserve exact integer G2 transforms, nine3x3 subkernels, two-row tile reorder and layer quantization; explicitly enable online replicate padding and exact IEEE output encoding. Test whether reducing duplicated conversion/top ownership overhead makes the unchanged Winograd compute schedule HLS-resource feasible. This advance paper plan is conditional: implementation/execution waits for the measured F28 loader conclusion, and missed WS2 gates cannot be called accepted.

## 2. Changes / 改动

Independent W17 copy. Replace top raw/transformed banks and load loops with nested parameter DATAFLOW: one read_raw_params converter, a single-channel81-coefficient transform buffer, and three distinct layer streams. Conv1 packets contain16 exact14-bit transformed fields (224bits),640 packets total including64 bias packets. Consumers load private simple-dual-port LUTRAM banks before pixels; inherit the measured-selected local-load flatten policy. Conv2/Conv3 compute and Winograd tile/window/reorder loops remain W17's. Use the frozen F25/F28 exact IEEE decode header. No parent design or interface change. Source commit: `7be52961ca1b0ed0b783afb3109d5de80d4d1ff6`.

## 6. Interpretation / 解读

W19 preserves both butterfly calls bit for bit against E017, the six-pixel border MSE, parameter-stream empty assertions and II1 in all three compute folds. Its 525,237-cycle HLS estimate is only 949 cycles above the 524,288-cycle Winograd tile-fold bound. This is a component scheduling estimate; finite-FIFO startup, raster reorder and repeated parameter loading still require RTL verification for any accepted successor.

The loader ownership hypothesis is partly confirmed: outside LUT falls from W17's 42,289 to 3,170, and the top multiplexer is 184 LUT. Total LUT falls from 93,166 to 83,623, a saving of 9,543; it does not fall by the whole change in outside attribution. The 80,453-LUT DATAFLOW region now owns the private banks and loading control. W19 remains infeasible because total LUT exceeds 81,984 by 1,639. Its DSP645, BRAM18K146 and FF130843 meet their respective unchanged limits; the 1.74903921ms HLS estimate cannot be ranked as an accepted speedup.

The new read_params process is 8,052 LUT, including a 3,895-LUT raw decoder/producer and 4,075-LUT transform_params1 child. The transform_groups report identifies nine sparsemux instances at 262 LUT each, 2,358 total, while its expression category is 625 LUT. These variable flat-cache selections are a concrete target for W21's independent subkernel-cache layout probe. Keep exact G2 arithmetic, transformed width, packet count and inference scheduling unchanged so the next area difference has a clear cause.

The parsed estimate audit shows no deviation above twenty percent in the requested DSP, memory, LUT, FF or cycle estimates. The advance 78,000-LUT budget was still optimistic enough to miss feasibility; future budgets must count measured private-bank control as well as MAC resources. Compare both direct-T8 controls, then validate only a measured resource-feasible successor. Missing comic and board validation remain outstanding.

Integer G2 preserves exact products and performs an exact divide-by-four before the original layer cast. Wider transforms and inverse additions, nine subkernels, runtime weight transforms and two raster row FIFOs are outside a multiplication-only budget. The128x128 tile grid computes65536 output positions before discarding the511 extra positions. PE/layer results do not establish full-network feasibility.
