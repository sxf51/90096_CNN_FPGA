## 6. Interpretation / 解读

All 13 course Set14 images match E065 exactly: the first image loads parameters and the next 12 reuse them. Mean deltaPSNR remains -0.07589037644656216 dB and butterfly six-pixel border MSE remains 1.405191888267716e-05. Independent C24, C25 and F53 cover the four-call ignored-pointer/reload/retention sequence, fresh-process forced loading and legacy regression.

The report retains 1032 DSP and 146 BRAM18K, exactly matching the paper budgets. Its 128840 LUT and 193984 FF are close to the planned 128900 and 193500; versus frozen C13 the load predicate adds 874 LUT and 1676 FF. All three continuous folds achieve II1. The 263090-cycle maximum and 262189-cycle minimum are HLS scheduling estimates, not measured loaded/reused transactions, so the paper's 300632 loaded cycles and 4500-11000 saved cycles still require RTL measurement. The HLS estimate is not a substitute for the planned per-call cycle comparison.

The top HLS clock estimate grows from C13's 2.431 ns to 2.920 ns. Parsed modules identify the read_raw_params pipeline, transform_bias and write_output at 2.920 ns; the MAC folds are 2.847/2.818/2.771 ns. These report estimates locate changed scheduling around the load/control paths but do not identify a routed critical path. HLS flags three 200-1614 streamed-input/predecessor deadlock risks plus 200-765/200-1450 output synchronization in read_raw_params; four-call finite-FIFO cosimulation must resolve those warnings empirically. The older weight-predecessor 200-1449 warning is absent. params_loaded is explicitly reported as power-on initialized; board software should still request loading after programming.

The original HLS LUT/FF gates retain status=infeasible. The user-approved routed policy admits this point only to screening, since 128840 LUT/193984 FF/1032 DSP fall below the current 160k/230k/1100 HLS screen. No physical feasibility, zero parameter AXI activity or WS-F speedup is established yet.

Subsequent C27 RTL observation rejects this version for the strict WS-F zero-parameter-AXI requirement: a skipped call still issues one AR request and one R beat, reading4bytes. HLS scalarizes biases3[0] and hoists that read into the outer top before the guarded DATAFLOW region. Preserve C23's original C/HLS evidence and C27's independent failure; do not route this version. C28-C30 use an independent design with guarded volatile scalar capture and must repeat the complete acceptance sequence.
