## 6. Interpretation / 解读

The `conv1_stream_csynth.rpt` FIFO table confirms `raster_U` depth256/width640/163840bits,0BRAM18K/347LUT/99FF, alongside the unchanged top/bottom row FIFOs. The independent forwarding process is present. Total81550LUT/130176FF adds464LUT/119FF to W21, while644DSP/146BRAM18K and all foldII1 remain unchanged. Deviations from84100LUT/131000FF paper totals are-3.03%/-0.63%, within20%.

The540000-cycle paper prediction requires RTL evidence; the unchanged525237-cycle HLS component bound cannot assess row-burst backpressure. F46 will measure the actual changed-parameter two-frame latency and the new raster FIFO counters. The report's347LUT for163840 logical FIFO bits is much lower than the2560LUT naive distributed-RAM budget. Treat that incremental discrepancy as HLS memory-model uncertainty until physical mapping is measured, not as evidence that the full buffer uses347 LUT on silicon.
