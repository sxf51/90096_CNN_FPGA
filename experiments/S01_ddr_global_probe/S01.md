## 6. Interpretation / 解读

Require both scratch AXI bundles before interpreting this as a DDR implementation. Compare ideal sequential cycles with reported memory-port stalls and report bandwidth lower bounds separately.

Whole-network DATAFLOW overlaps the dedicated layer engines. Tile fusion removes full feature maps but executes Conv1/Conv2 and Conv3 sequentially, recomputes halos, and can lose memory parallelism. Requested AXI burst limits are upper bounds; only inferred bursts are evidence of burst generation. DDR payload/bandwidth models exclude protocol stalls and are lower bounds.
