## 6. Interpretation / 解读

W32 passes every independent routed gate at4.5ns: WNS+0.144ns, TNS0, WHS+0.029ns, no unrouted or partially routed nets,644DSP/45030LUT/89096FF/109BRAM18K/zeroURAM. The same W21 RTL was already validated by F97's changed-parameter two-frame simulation at558758/558787cycles and by F96 on all13 images with deltaPSNR=-0.0695141811880653dB. The IP frame time is2.514411ms, equal to F52 and9.090909% below W22. This is an ideal-interface IP result; board DDR, drivers and system interconnect remain unmeasured.

The preplanned W22 physical resource estimates644DSP/42592LUT/88806FF/109BRAM18K are close to W32. Vivado's Performance_Explore strategy and post-route physical optimization add control replication/physical mapping cost but improve setup margin.
