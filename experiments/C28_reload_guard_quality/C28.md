## 6. Interpretation / 解读

All13 course Set14 images are E065-exact: the first loads parameters and the other12 reuse the banks. Mean deltaPSNR remains-0.07589037644656216dB, and butterfly six-pixel border MSE remains1.405191888267716e-05. Independent C29 confirms load/reuse/reload/latest-bank C semantics; C30 confirms first-call load=0 forced loading in a fresh process.

Actual HLS1032DSP/146BRAM18K/0URAM/128849LUT/193984FF closely matches the paper1032/146/0/129000/194150 budgets. Versus C23 the guard adds9LUT and no DSP, BRAM or FF; the three folds remain II1 with depths8/11/13 for Winograd/Conv2/Conv3. The estimated clock remains2.920ns and maximum bound remains263090cycles. These unchanged HLS numbers do not measure loaded or reused RTL transactions. The paper300632loaded cycles and289632-296132reuse range must be compared with C31's actual transactions.

Generated top RTL changes the entry transition: when do_load is false, state1 goes directly to state11 instead of state2, bypassing the scalar-bias AR/R states. do_load is the OR of load_params and the inverse persistent params_loaded flag. This static inspection supports the repair hypothesis but is not zero-AXI acceptance. Three200-1614 streamed-input/predecessor warnings remain, and params_loaded is reported power-on initialized; four-call finite-FIFO RTL is required to verify progress and retained weights.

The original HLS resource verdict remains infeasible because LUT/FF exceed the earlier70percent gates. Current routed-policy screening passes128849LUT/193984FF/1032DSP against160k/230k/1100, permitting independent hardware verification without declaring physical feasibility. C27 releases the sole heavy slot after its preserved AXI failure. C31 repeats four exact calls, zero skipped parameter AR/R/AW/W activity, latency ordering and unchanged synthesis RTL hashes; only its accepted result permits a new independently staged5ns implementation.
