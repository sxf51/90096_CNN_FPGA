Retain the original srcnn wrapper with load=true and add srcnn_reload with a control load_params bit. Force the first call to load even when its flag isfalse; skipped calls must ignore all incoming parameter pointers and reuse the most recent persistent banks. ## 6. Interpretation / 解读

Normal butterfly matches the E065 mixed reference, skipped-load lenna matches normal-weight lenna despite constructed incoming parameters, reloading the constructed model yields 104/256 at every pixel, and a final skipped call retains 104/256 despite incoming zero parameters. This is a C-only sequence: DSP, physical memory, cycles and AXI traffic remain unmeasured, so the paper hardware estimates have no actual comparator. C23 separately checks the complete 13-image quality set; C25 separately checks fresh-process cold start. Advance to four-transaction RTL only after those checks, the legacy regression and HLS screening all pass, then require observed zero parameter AXI traffic on skipped calls and unchanged synthesis RTL hashes.
