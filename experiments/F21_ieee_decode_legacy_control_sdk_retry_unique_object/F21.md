## 6. Interpretation / 解读

Compare exact float bits,II1,DSP and converter clock. A zero-DSP claim requires the actual report. Only integrate a passing exact microkernel into a new full-network design.

Continuous scheduling separates window generation and MAC folds, shifts channel registers and explicitly registers fabric reduction trees. This can remove pixel fill/drain but costs pipeline registers and wide window FIFOs. Online padding overlaps source reads with padded writes; old row serialization can create finite-FIFO stalls absent from maximum-component HLS estimates. RTL cycles and routed slack remain the final evidence.

Explicit explanation of deviations above20%:

Converter planning allowances reserved selection/control margin. Exact IEEE construction operates on a ten-bit magnitude and highest-set-bit selector, then assigns binary32 exponent/significand fields; its finite range lets synthesis remove unused selector states and registers. The legacy control retains integer-to-float and float scaling operators. These PE array-memory ports also omit the full network's AXI address/handshake logic, so PE resource differences cannot be subtracted blindly from a frame estimate.
