## 6. Interpretation / 解读

The direct T8 control is exact on both butterfly calls and retains the original border MSE. All three continuous fold loops achieve II1. The 525,761-cycle HLS estimate is 5,561 cycles above the 520,200-cycle pixel-fold bound and the DSP split is exactly 648/256/100, matching the 1,004-DSP paper budget.

The registered-tree variant remains infeasible on two independent gates: 87,869 LUT exceeds 81,984, and 194,668 FF exceeds 163,968. Private parameter ownership reduces outside LUT to 3,170 and top multiplexers to 184, but the DATAFLOW region is now 84,699 LUT and 191,458 FF. Moving the ownership boundary cannot establish total-area feasibility. The resource estimates differ from the advance budget by less than twenty percent, yet the measured FF count is worse than its already-infeasible 169,000 estimate.

Conv1 contributes 37,394 LUT/101,486 FF, Conv2 14,749 LUT/39,414 FF and Conv3 23,557 LUT/43,715 FF in their complete layer reports. Their child fold and load reports are included in those counts and must not be added twice. The registered reduction and wide held windows account for substantial register demand even after shared decoding. F30 changes only the reduction addition latency and is the matched test of whether saving those registers is sufficient; retain every resource gate and the actual clock/II results.

At the common 3.33ns target, this point's 1.75078413ms estimate is similar to W19's 1.74903921ms while requiring 359 more DSPs and 63,825 more FF. Both points are infeasible, so this comparison describes measured architecture costs rather than an accepted acceleration. Keep F28 as the exact, HLS-resource-feasible fallback until a T8 point passes all gates and candidate-specific image/RTL/route verification.

Continuous scheduling separates window generation and MAC folds, shifts channel registers and explicitly registers fabric reduction trees. This can remove pixel fill/drain but costs pipeline registers and wide window FIFOs. Online padding overlaps source reads with padded writes; old row serialization can create finite-FIFO stalls absent from maximum-component HLS estimates. RTL cycles and routed slack remain the final evidence.
