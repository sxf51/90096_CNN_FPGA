## 6. Interpretation / 解读

Compare all three variants at identical precision, T32 and10ns. Separate partial-sum, pad-row, FIFO and merged-weight memory; explain estimates differing by over20 percent using per-module loops/resources.

Scatter trades the channel-wide gather line/window storage for five32-bit partial rows and a5x5 register window. Explicit padding retains a32-channel row; merged edge kernels remove it but introduce nine coefficient classes, wider coefficients, selection and runtime merge work. Equality of every butterfly output, including six-pixel borders and the repeated frame, is the padding gate.
