# F26: s2p_bias_shift_T16

## 1. Purpose and hypothesis / 目的与假设

WS2 control: isolate whether simple-dual-port runtime weight banks and constant-index bias shift loading remove top-level bank port multiplexers and dynamic register-write decoding. Keep the frozen F25 arithmetic, online replicate padding, two frames, AXI signature, T16 and 2.5ns clock. Expect identical output and three II1 folds; bank ownership remains shared, so the outside-LUT target may not be reached by this control alone.
