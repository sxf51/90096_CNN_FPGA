## 6. Interpretation / 解读

W21 is the first HLS-resource-feasible T8 point in this workstream. It preserves both butterfly calls bit for bit, the original borderMSE and all three foldII1 results. TotalLUT81086 is898 below81984;644DSP,146BRAM18K and130057FF meet their unchanged limits. Estimated clock remains2.431ns and the525237-cycle HLS frame bound gives1.74903921ms at the3.33ns target. Missing comic still makes quality acceptance provisional, and HLS does not establish RTL progress or routed timing.

The cache-layout hypothesis is confirmed by the matched W19 control: transform_params1 falls from4075 to1538LUT, exactly the2537-LUT reduction of the whole design. Outside3170LUT and the top184-LUT multiplexer remain unchanged. The transform_groups child falls from3055 to1056LUT; its nine sparsemux_123 instances at262LUT each become nine sparsemux_19 instances at49LUT each,2358 to441LUT. The removed affine indexing also eliminates the transformer's one DSP, bringing the total to the exact288/256/100 MAC split. Loading is176LUT in the new columns child versus808LUT in the old flat-tap child; parent control/holding registers must be counted in their complete module totals.

The selected-mux sub-budget was270LUT and actual441 is63.3% higher: HLS still generates19-input sparse forms, rather than the assumed nine-choice selectors. Additional savings in load indexing and control offset that miss, so totalLUT is0.75% below the81700 estimate. The requested whole-design DSP, memory, FF and cycle estimates have no deviation above twenty percent; retain the local selector miss in the estimate interpretation instead of rewriting the paper budget.

The transformer's own scheduled latency grows from6596 to8516cycles, a29.1% increase caused by the explicitly separate nine row-load pipelines: the new row nest costs117cycles per81 coefficients, and a channel iteration costs132 instead of102. The top HLS bound remains unchanged because the much longer compute component dominates its DATAFLOW estimate. This does not show that the finite-FIFO startup cost is free; candidate-specific RTL must measure it and verify parameter reload, tile/raster ordering and deadlock freedom.

Select W21 for matching-source validation on every supplied image, changed-parameter two-frame RTL and its own serial packaged implementation. Keep F28 as the strict T16 fallback and retain W19/F29/F30 as failed resource controls. The898-LUT margin is small; future clock or control changes need their own resource evidence. Use measured routed paths for WS4 and preserve the runtime float parameter interface, quantization and HLS gates.

Integer G2 preserves exact products and performs an exact divide-by-four before the original layer cast. Wider transforms and inverse additions, nine subkernels, runtime weight transforms and two raster row FIFOs are outside a multiplication-only budget. The128x128 tile grid computes65536 output positions before discarding the511 extra positions. PE/layer results do not establish full-network feasibility.
