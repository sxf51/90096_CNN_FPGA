# F25: nested_weight_loader_T16_2p5ns

## 1. Purpose and hypothesis / 目的与假设

F24 eliminates F23's address multiplier and reports2.830503377ms, but its automatically flattened channel/row/column loop has a2.699ns kx compare/and/select/add/store control path. The unchanged inference region remains2.377ns. Disable flattening only in the Conv1 weight loader to isolate the small nine-element inner counter and test whether that control path shortens. Keep the same float weight contract, T16 engines and exact quantization. Accept additional per-row pipeline drain only if its measured frame/clock product improves.
